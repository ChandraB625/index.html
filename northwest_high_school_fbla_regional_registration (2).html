Connect Google Sheet in 3 Steps

Send form submissions directly into your Google Spreadsheet in real time.

Step 1: Open Google Sheets

Create a Google Sheet titled "NWHS FBLA Regionals Sign-ups 2026". Add these column headers in row 1:

Timestamp | FullName | Grade | Email | Phone | PaymentMethod | IndividualEvent | TeamEvent | Teammates | Dietary | ParentName | ParentPhone

Step 2: Paste Google Apps Script

Click Extensions > Apps Script in your sheet, delete any existing code, and paste this exact script:

function doPost(e) {
  var sheet = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().getActiveSheet();
  var data = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);
  sheet.appendRow([
    new Date(),
    data.fullName,
    data.gradeLevel,
    data.email,
    data.phone,
    data.paymentMethod,
    data.individualEvent,
    data.teamEvent,
    data.teammates,
    data.dietaryRestrictions,
    data.parentName,
    data.parentPhone
  ]);
  return ContentService.createTextOutput("Success").setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.TEXT);
}

Step 3: Deploy as Web App & Paste URL Below

Click Deploy > New Deployment, choose type Web App, set Who has access to "Anyone", click Deploy, and copy the Web App URL.

Bypassing "Google hasn't verified this app": When authorizing your script, Google displays this standard warning for user-created scripts. To proceed:
  1. Click "Advanced" in the bottom-left corner of the Google pop-up.
  2. Click "Go to [Script Name] (unsafe)" at the bottom.
  3. Click "Allow" to grant access to write to your Google Sheet.
